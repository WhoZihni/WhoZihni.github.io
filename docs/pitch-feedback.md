# Elevator Pitch — Feedback

## Environment
- Simple background is good, but low camera angle shows ceiling — not ideal for professional presentation
- Avoid distracting objects in the background

## Camera Angle
- Low-angle shot is not ideal. Place camera at eye level for a more natural connection with the viewer
- Distance from camera looks good

## Presentation
- Black t-shirt is fine but consider a polo or button-up if possible

## Script Content
- Avoid mentioning age — let projects and experience speak
- Instead of "finishing" bootcamp, use wording that shows progression and continued growth
- Don't list every technology — keep it general, mention only a few examples (e.g. "frontend and backend with Java and React")
- Focus on what you built and the problems you solved — more impactful than listing courses or tools
- Clearly state what type of opportunity you're looking for at the end

## Delivery
- Speak a little slower, add small pauses between ideas
- Maintain eye contact with the camera
- Smile naturally — creates stronger connection

## Overall
- Strongest point: focusing on real projects and real problems
- Solid introduction, needs minor refinements to feel more professional
